1. PURPOSE
This Agreement sets out the terms under which Seventh Sky will provide Residential Property Purchase Services to assist the Client in identifying, assessing, negotiating and coordinating the purchase of a residential property.
The Agreement establishes the rights and responsibilities of both Parties in relation to property search, inspections, negotiations, documentation coordination, settlement support and other agreed purchase services.
The specific services, pricing and engagement scope applicable to each transaction will be confirmed in the approved Quotation, Work Order or Property Purchase Schedule.
2. TERM
This Agreement becomes effective on the date signed by both Parties and continues until:
the property purchase is completed;
the agreed Services have been completed;
the Agreement is terminated in accordance with this Agreement; or
both Parties otherwise agree in writing.
Where the Client engages Seventh Sky on an exclusive basis, the exclusive engagement period shall be specified in the approved Quotation or Work Order.

4. BUYER REQUIREMENTS
The Client's purchasing requirements shall be recorded in Schedule B or the approved Property Purchase Schedule.
The information may include:
Preferred Property Type;
Preferred Location(s);
Intended Use (Owner Occupier / Investment);
Budget Range;
Preferred Property Size;
Finance Method;
Expected Purchase Timeframe;
Special Requirements;
Selected Services; and
Other agreed purchasing criteria.
Where any inconsistency exists between this Agreement and the approved Work Order or Property Purchase Schedule, the approved Work Order or Property Purchase Schedule shall prevail for that transaction.
5. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES
The Client agrees to:
provide accurate personal, financial and purchasing information;
provide truthful information regarding financing capability and purchase readiness;
provide all documents reasonably required to perform the Services;
cooperate with inspections, meetings and negotiations;
promptly notify Seventh Sky of any changes to purchasing requirements or financial circumstances;
pay all agreed fees and approved third-party expenses in accordance with this Agreement;
obtain independent legal, taxation, valuation, engineering and financial advice where appropriate;
independently assess whether a property is suitable before entering into a purchase contract; and
act honestly and in good faith throughout the purchase process.
The Client acknowledges that they remain responsible for:
making the final purchasing decision;
obtaining finance approval;
satisfying themselves regarding the property's condition;
reviewing all legal documentation before purchase; and
complying with all applicable laws relating to the property purchase.
6. RESPONSIBILITIES OF SEVENTH SKY
Seventh Sky will:
provide the agreed Services with reasonable care and skill;
identify suitable properties based on the Client's requirements;
coordinate inspections and property viewings;
communicate with sellers and their representatives where authorised;
assist with negotiations where requested;
coordinate with lawyers, conveyancers, valuers, surveyors, banks and other approved professionals where required;
keep the Client reasonably informed of significant developments throughout the purchase process;
provide progress updates during the engagement; and
coordinate settlement activities where included within the agreed Services.
Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Seventh Sky acts as the Property Purchase Coordinator and Service Provider and does not act as the seller, legal adviser, conveyancer, valuer, surveyor, engineer or financial adviser.
7. QUOTATIONS & WORK ORDERS
Before commencing the Services, Seventh Sky may issue a Quotation or Work Order specifying:
the agreed Services;
engagement type (exclusive or non-exclusive);
professional fees;
success fee or commission (if applicable);
payment schedule;
expected project timeframe; and
any special conditions.
Additional services requested after commencement may require a revised Quotation, Work Order or approved variation before work proceeds.
8. SERVICE FEES & PAYMENT
The fees payable under this Agreement shall be specified in the approved Quotation, Work Order or Property Purchase Schedule.
Depending on the selected services, fees may include:
consultation fees;
property search and shortlisting;
market research and property assessment;
inspection coordination;
negotiation support;
documentation coordination;
settlement coordination;
professional service fees;
success fee (where applicable);
approved third-party service fees; and
any other agreed services.
The Client agrees to pay all agreed fees in accordance with the approved payment schedule.
Government charges, registration fees, taxes, legal fees, conveyancing fees, property valuation fees, survey fees, bank charges, loan processing fees and other approved third-party expenses remain the responsibility of the Client unless otherwise agreed in writing.
9. PAYMENT TERMS
Unless otherwise agreed in writing:
Property Purchase Services
Deposit upon acceptance of the Quotation or Work Order (where applicable).
Progress payments for approved milestone services (if applicable).
Final payment upon completion of the agreed Services or settlement of the property purchase, whichever occurs first.
Success Fee Engagement
Where the Parties agree to a success fee arrangement, the agreed success fee shall become payable upon successful completion of the property purchase and settlement.
Invoices are payable within the period stated on the invoice.
Late payments may result in suspension of the Services.
10. PROPERTY PURCHASE SERVICES
Seventh Sky will use reasonable efforts to identify residential properties that meet the Client's stated requirements.
The Client acknowledges that:
suitable properties are subject to market availability;
asking prices and market values may change without notice;
sellers have the right to accept, reject or withdraw offers;
successful purchase of a property cannot be guaranteed; and
the final decision to purchase any property remains solely with the Client.
Where requested, Seventh Sky may coordinate:
property searches;
property inspections;
seller communication;
offer preparation;
negotiations;
documentation coordination;
settlement coordination; and
post-purchase support services.
11. PROJECT TIMELINES
Estimated commencement and completion dates will be provided in the approved Quotation or Work Order.
Project timeframes may be affected by:
property availability;
market conditions;
seller response times;
finance approval;
legal documentation;
valuation or survey requirements;
settlement arrangements;
government processing;
public holidays; or
other circumstances beyond Seventh Sky's reasonable control.
Where delays occur, Seventh Sky will keep the Client reasonably informed and provide an updated estimated timeframe where reasonably practicable.
12. VARIATIONS & ADDITIONAL SERVICES
A variation occurs where the Client requests work outside the approved scope of Services or where additional work becomes necessary.
Examples include:
additional property searches;
inspections outside the agreed scope;
extended negotiations;
additional valuation or survey coordination;
legal documentation coordination;
finance or loan assistance coordination;
relocation assistance;
interior design consultation coordination;
utility connection coordination; or
any other Client-requested services.
Where practical, Seventh Sky will advise the Client of:
the additional work;
any additional fees; and
any expected impact on the project timeline.
No variation will proceed without the Client's approval unless immediate action is reasonably necessary to protect the Client's interests or facilitate the purchase process.
13. PROPERTY PURCHASE COORDINATION & THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
Where requested, Seventh Sky may coordinate inspections, negotiations and communication with property sellers and approved third-party professionals.
The Client acknowledges that:
property information is obtained from sellers, developers, agents and other sources reasonably believed to be reliable at the time;
Seventh Sky does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of information supplied by third parties;
lawyers, conveyancers, valuers, surveyors, engineers, banks, financial institutions and other independent professionals remain responsible for their own advice and services;
the Client remains responsible for carrying out appropriate due diligence and deciding whether to proceed with the purchase; and
Seventh Sky remains responsible for coordinating the agreed Services with reasonable care and skill.
14. SERVICE COMPLETION
The Services shall be considered completed when the agreed scope of work specified in the Quotation, Work Order or Property Purchase Schedule has been substantially delivered.
Depending on the selected services, completion may include:
property search completed;
suitable properties identified and presented;
inspections coordinated;
negotiations coordinated;
documentation coordinated;
settlement completed;
post-purchase coordination completed; or
any other agreed Services completed.
Where Seventh Sky is engaged for ongoing purchase support services, completion shall occur upon the expiry of the agreed engagement period unless renewed by mutual agreement.
15. CLIENT COMPLAINTS & SERVICE ISSUES
If the Client believes that the Services have not been delivered in accordance with the agreed scope, the Client should notify Seventh Sky as soon as reasonably practicable.
Upon receiving a complaint, Seventh Sky will:
acknowledge the complaint;
investigate the matter;
liaise with relevant parties where appropriate;
take reasonable steps to resolve the issue;
keep the Client informed of the outcome; and
implement corrective action where reasonably appropriate.
Where the issue arises from Seventh Sky's administrative oversight, reasonable efforts will be made to rectify the matter promptly.
Nothing in this Agreement limits any rights available to the Client under applicable law.
16. LIABILITY
To the extent permitted by the laws of Bangladesh:
Seventh Sky will provide the agreed Services with reasonable care and skill.
Seventh Sky is responsible only for the Services it has agreed to provide under this Agreement.
The Client remains responsible for making the final decision to purchase any property.
The Client remains responsible for obtaining finance approval, reviewing legal documentation and completing appropriate due diligence before entering into a purchase contract.
Property sellers remain responsible for the accuracy of the information and disclosures they provide regarding the property.
Independent professionals, including lawyers, conveyancers, valuers, surveyors, engineers, banks, financial institutions and tax advisers, remain responsible for their own advice and services.
Neither Party shall be liable for delays or failures resulting from circumstances beyond their reasonable control.
17. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Except where liability cannot legally be excluded or limited, Seventh Sky shall not be liable for:
a seller deciding not to proceed with the sale;
the Client deciding not to proceed with the purchase;
delays caused by finance approval, legal documentation, settlement processes or regulatory requirements;
changes in property values or market conditions;
inaccurate or incomplete information provided by sellers, developers or other third parties;
structural defects, hidden defects or matters not reasonably identifiable during the coordination of the Services;
the actions or omissions of sellers, contractors or independent professionals;
indirect or consequential loss, including loss of opportunity, loss of profit or financial loss arising from matters beyond Seventh Sky's reasonable control; or
events beyond Seventh Sky's reasonable control.
Where permitted by law, Seventh Sky's total liability arising from the Services shall not exceed the amount paid by the Client for the affected Services.
This clause does not exclude liability arising from fraud, wilful misconduct or any liability that cannot legally be excluded.
18. CONFIDENTIALITY & PRIVACY
Each Party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information obtained through this Agreement.
Personal and property information will only be collected, used and disclosed for:
providing the agreed Services;
identifying and assessing suitable properties;
communicating with the Client, sellers and authorised representatives;
coordinating with lawyers, conveyancers, valuers, surveyors, banks and other authorised third parties;
preparing quotations, Work Orders, inspection reports and invoices;
complying with legal obligations; or
other purposes authorised by the Client.
Neither Party shall disclose confidential information to any third party without prior consent unless required by law.
These obligations continue after completion or termination of this Agreement.
19. FORCE MAJEURE
Neither Party shall be responsible for delays or failure to perform caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including:
natural disasters;
severe weather;
flood or fire;
government restrictions;
industrial action;
civil unrest;
pandemics;
major technology failures;
financial system disruptions; or
other unforeseen events.
The affected Party shall notify the other Party as soon as reasonably practicable and resume performance once the event has ended.
20. BUYER ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS
The Client acknowledges that:
they have reviewed and accepted the Quotation or Work Order before the Services commence;
property availability and purchase opportunities depend on market conditions;
the final decision to purchase any property remains solely with the Client;
finance approval, legal due diligence and settlement remain the responsibility of the Client and their appointed professional advisers;
property information supplied by sellers or third parties should be independently verified before purchase;
some Services involve coordination with independent third-party professionals;
independent legal, taxation, financial, engineering and building inspection advice should be obtained before entering into a purchase contract; and
they have had the opportunity to ask questions and obtain independent professional advice before entering into this Agreement.
21. SUSPENSION & TERMINATION
21.1 Suspension
Seventh Sky may suspend the Services where:
the Client fails to make payment in accordance with this Agreement;
the Client fails to provide information or documents reasonably required to perform the Services;
the Client provides false, misleading or fraudulent information;
the Client requests suspension; or
continuation of the Services would be unlawful or impracticable.
Where practicable, Seventh Sky will notify the Client before suspending the Services.
21.2 Termination
Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days' written notice, provided all completed Services and outstanding payments have been settled.
Seventh Sky may terminate this Agreement immediately where the Client:
commits a material breach of this Agreement;
provides false or misleading financial or purchasing information;
fails to make payment after receiving written notice;
engages in unlawful or fraudulent conduct; or
prevents Seventh Sky from performing the agreed Services.
Where the Client terminates after Services have commenced, the Client remains responsible for payment for:
Services already completed;
property search and inspection coordination already undertaken;
negotiations already conducted;
approved third-party costs incurred; and
other reasonable expenses incurred before termination.
Termination does not affect any rights or obligations that arose before the termination date.
22. NON-CIRCUMVENTION
The Client acknowledges that Seventh Sky invests significant time, expertise and commercial resources in identifying suitable properties, coordinating inspections and introducing purchase opportunities.
Accordingly, where Seventh Sky introduces a property, seller or authorised representative to the Client, the Client agrees that, during the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months after the introduction, the Client will not intentionally bypass Seventh Sky to negotiate or complete the purchase directly with the introduced seller without Seventh Sky's prior written consent.
Where the Client purchases a property introduced by Seventh Sky during this period, the agreed professional fees or success fee specified in the approved Quotation or Work Order shall remain payable.
This clause does not prevent the Client from purchasing a property that was not introduced by Seventh Sky.
23. GENERAL PROVISIONS
Unless otherwise agreed in writing:
this Agreement, together with the accepted Quotation, Work Order and Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties;
any amendment must be made in writing and signed or electronically accepted by both Parties;
failure by either Party to enforce any provision does not waive its rights;
if any provision is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect; and
notices under this Agreement may be delivered by hand, courier, registered post or email.
24. GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.
The Parties agree to make reasonable efforts to resolve any dispute through good faith discussions before commencing legal proceedings.
If the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation, the Parties may agree to mediation or another recognised alternative dispute resolution process before referring the matter to the competent courts of Bangladesh.
Nothing in this clause prevents either Party from seeking urgent legal or equitable relief where necessary.
25. EXECUTION
By signing this Agreement, the Parties confirm that they:
have read and understood this Agreement;
have had the opportunity to obtain independent legal, financial, taxation and professional advice;
enter into this Agreement voluntarily; and
agree to be legally bound by its terms.
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, including through approved electronic signature platforms such as DocuSign. Each signed copy shall be deemed an original and together constitute one Agreement.
