1. PURPOSE
This Agreement sets out the terms under which Seventh Sky will coordinate and manage Business Registration Services requested by the Client.
Depending on the nature of the project, services may be provided directly by Seventh Sky or by qualified third-party professionals appointed or coordinated by Seventh Sky.
The specific services, pricing and project requirements for each engagement will be confirmed in the relevant Quotation and Work Order.
2. TERM
This Agreement becomes effective on the date it is signed by both Parties and continues until:
all agreed Services have been completed;
all outstanding payments have been made; and
all agreed deliverables have been provided,
unless terminated earlier under this Agreement.
Where ongoing corporate compliance or annual filing services are engaged, this Agreement continues for the period specified in the approved Work Order.

5. RESPONSIBILITIES OF SEVENTH SKY
Seventh Sky will:
coordinate the requested services;
provide quotations and Work Orders;
coordinate qualified third-party professionals where required;
monitor project progress;
keep the Client reasonably informed;
coordinate communication with relevant stakeholders where applicable;
manage project administration and documentation; and
endeavour to ensure the Services are delivered professionally and within agreed timeframes.
Unless expressly stated otherwise, Seventh Sky acts as the project coordinator and contract administrator and does not act as a government authority or guarantee approvals issued by any government department or regulatory body.
6. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES
The Client agrees to:
provide complete and accurate information;
provide genuine supporting documents;
promptly provide additional information when requested;
review and approve quotations before work commences;
promptly advise Seventh Sky of any changes affecting the project;
pay all agreed fees in accordance with this Agreement;
pay applicable government fees and statutory charges; and
cooperate throughout the registration process.
Delays caused by the Client may result in revised project timelines or additional costs.
7. QUOTATIONS & WORK ORDERS
Before any work begins, Seventh Sky will provide the Client with a Quotation and, where applicable, a Work Order outlining:
selected services;
scope of work;
estimated project timeline;
agreed pricing;
payment schedule;
expected deliverables; and
any special conditions.
Work will commence only after the Client accepts the Quotation or Work Order.
If the Client requests additional services after approval, a revised quotation or variation may be required.
8. SERVICE FEES & PAYMENT
The total project cost will be specified in the approved Quotation or Work Order.
Project fees may include:
consultation fees;
professional service fees;
documentation preparation;
registration coordination;
government liaison;
regulatory filing support;
administrative charges;
approved third-party professional fees (where applicable);
government fees and statutory charges (where applicable); and
any approved additional services.
The Client agrees to pay the agreed amount in accordance with the approved payment schedule.
Government fees, stamp duties, taxes and statutory charges are payable by the Client unless otherwise stated in the approved Quotation or Work Order.
9. PAYMENT TERMS
Unless otherwise agreed in writing:
Registration & Documentation Services
Deposit upon acceptance of the Quotation or Work Order.
Progress payment (where applicable).
Final payment upon completion of the agreed Services or submission of the relevant application.
Ongoing Compliance Services
Where the Client engages Seventh Sky for ongoing compliance services (such as annual returns or regulatory filing support), payment may be made:
☐ Monthly
☐ Quarterly
☐ Half-Yearly
☐ Annually
as specified in the approved Work Order.
Invoices are payable within the agreed payment period stated on the invoice.
Late payments may result in suspension or delay of the Services.
10. GOVERNMENT AUTHORITIES & REGULATORY APPROVALS
The Client acknowledges that:
registration approvals are issued solely by the relevant government authority;
Seventh Sky cannot guarantee approval of any application;
government processing times may vary;
government authorities may request additional information or supporting documents;
approval, rejection or delay remains at the discretion of the relevant authority.
Where additional information is requested, Seventh Sky will use reasonable efforts to coordinate the response with the Client.
11. PROJECT TIMELINES
Estimated commencement and completion dates will be provided in the approved Quotation or Work Order.
Project timeframes may be affected by:
government processing times;
availability of required documents;
delays by the Client;
regulatory reviews;
public holidays;
system outages affecting government agencies;
legislative changes; or
other circumstances beyond Seventh Sky's reasonable control.
Where delays occur, Seventh Sky will keep the Client reasonably informed and provide an updated estimated completion timeframe where reasonably practicable.
12. VARIATIONS & ADDITIONAL SERVICES
A variation occurs where the Client requests work outside the approved scope of Services or where additional work becomes necessary.
Examples include:
additional registrations;
amendments to submitted applications;
additional company documentation;
shareholder or director changes;
regulatory responses;
additional government submissions;
certified document preparation;
translation services; or
additional Client requests.
Where practical, Seventh Sky will advise the Client of:
the additional work;
any additional charges; and
any impact on the project timeline.
No variation will proceed without the Client's approval unless immediate action is reasonably necessary to protect the Client's interests or comply with legal requirements.
13. PROFESSIONAL SERVICES & THIRD-PARTY PROVIDERS
The Client acknowledges that certain Services may be performed or supported by qualified professionals, consultants or specialist service providers appointed or coordinated by Seventh Sky.
Unless otherwise agreed in writing:
professional advisers remain responsible for the advice they provide;
government authorities remain responsible for registration decisions;
third-party professionals remain responsible for their own professional services; and
Seventh Sky remains responsible for coordinating the project and assisting the Client throughout the engagement.
14. DOCUMENTATION, WARRANTIES & COMPLETION
Seventh Sky will use reasonable care and skill in coordinating the Services under this Agreement.
Upon completion of the agreed Services, Seventh Sky will provide the Client with the relevant documents or confirmation of submission, as applicable.
Where Services involve third-party professionals or government authorities:
professional advice remains the responsibility of the relevant professional;
government approvals remain the responsibility of the relevant authority;
official registration certificates and licences are issued by the relevant authority; and
statutory processing remains subject to applicable laws and government procedures.
Any warranty relating to third-party professional services shall remain subject to that provider's terms and conditions.
15. CLIENT COMPLAINTS & SERVICE ISSUES
If the Client believes that the Services have not been completed in accordance with the approved scope, the Client should notify Seventh Sky as soon as reasonably practicable.
Seventh Sky will:
acknowledge the complaint;
investigate the matter;
liaise with the relevant service provider or authority where required;
take reasonable steps to resolve the issue; and
keep the Client informed of the outcome.
Where errors arise due to Seventh Sky's administrative oversight, reasonable efforts will be made to rectify the matter promptly.
Nothing in this Agreement limits any rights available to the Client under applicable law.
16. LIABILITY
To the extent permitted by the laws of Bangladesh:
Seventh Sky is responsible for coordinating and administering the Services with reasonable care and skill.
Professional advice remains the responsibility of the qualified consultant or professional providing that advice.
Government authorities remain responsible for approvals, registrations, licences and statutory decisions.
Neither Party shall be liable for delays or failures resulting from circumstances beyond their reasonable control.
17. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Except where liability cannot legally be excluded or limited, Seventh Sky shall not be liable for:
delays caused by government authorities;
rejection of applications by government authorities;
inaccurate or incomplete information supplied by the Client;
actions or decisions of third-party professionals;
legislative or regulatory changes;
indirect or consequential losses;
loss of business opportunity, profit or goodwill; or
events beyond Seventh Sky's reasonable control.
Where permitted by law, Seventh Sky's total liability arising from a project shall not exceed the amount paid by the Client for the affected Services.
This clause does not exclude liability arising from fraud, wilful misconduct or any liability that cannot legally be excluded.
18. INSURANCE
Where appropriate, Seventh Sky may assist the Client in coordinating optional insurance or professional indemnity arrangements through third-party providers.
Any insurance:
is subject to the insurer's acceptance;
is governed by the insurer's policy terms;
does not guarantee approval of a claim; and
is separate from this Agreement.
The Client remains responsible for obtaining any insurance they consider necessary unless otherwise agreed in writing.
19. CONFIDENTIALITY & PRIVACY
Each Party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information obtained through this Agreement.
Personal information will only be collected, used and disclosed for:
providing the requested Services;
communicating with the Client;
preparing quotations, applications, invoices and Work Orders;
complying with legal and regulatory obligations; or
other purposes authorised by the Client.
Neither Party shall disclose confidential information to any third party without prior consent unless required by law.
These obligations continue after completion or termination of this Agreement.
20. FORCE MAJEURE
Neither Party shall be responsible for delays or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including:
natural disasters;
government restrictions;
industrial action;
civil unrest;
pandemics;
cyber incidents affecting government systems;
major technology failures; or
other unforeseen events.
The affected Party shall notify the other Party as soon as reasonably practicable and resume performance when reasonably possible.
21. CLIENT ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS
The Client acknowledges that:
they have reviewed and accepted the Quotation or Work Order before the Services commence;
government approvals, registrations and licences remain at the discretion of the relevant authority;
project timelines are estimates only and may vary depending on government processing;
additional information or documentation may be requested by government authorities;
some Services may be delivered by qualified independent professionals coordinated by Seventh Sky; and
they have had the opportunity to ask questions and obtain independent legal or professional advice before signing this Agreement.
22. SUSPENSION & TERMINATION
22.1 Suspension
Seventh Sky may suspend the Services where:
the Client fails to make payments in accordance with this Agreement;
the Client fails to provide required information or supporting documents;
the Client provides false, misleading or incomplete information;
continuation of the Services would breach applicable laws or regulatory requirements;
the Client requests suspension; or
continuation of the Services would be unlawful or impracticable.
Where practicable, Seventh Sky will notify the Client before suspending the Services.
22.2 Termination
Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice, provided all completed work and outstanding payments are settled.
Seventh Sky may terminate this Agreement immediately if the Client:
commits a material breach of this Agreement;
provides false or fraudulent information;
refuses to cooperate with reasonable information requests;
fails to make payment after receiving written notice; or
requests Services that would breach applicable laws or regulatory requirements.
The Client may terminate this Agreement before work commences by providing written notice.
Where work has already commenced, the Client remains responsible for payment for:
Services already performed;
professional time incurred;
documentation prepared;
government applications already lodged;
approved third-party costs incurred; and
any reasonable expenses incurred before termination.
Termination does not affect any rights or obligations that arose before the termination date.
23. GENERAL PROVISIONS
Unless otherwise agreed in writing:
this Agreement, together with the accepted Quotation, Work Order and Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties;
any amendment must be made in writing and signed or electronically accepted by both Parties;
failure by either Party to enforce a provision does not waive its rights;
if any provision is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect; and
notices under this Agreement may be delivered by hand, courier, registered post or email.
24. GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.
The Parties agree to make reasonable efforts to resolve any dispute through good faith discussions before commencing legal proceedings.
If the dispute cannot be resolved through negotiation, the Parties may agree to mediation or another recognised alternative dispute resolution process before referring the matter to the competent courts of Bangladesh.
Nothing in this clause prevents either Party from seeking urgent legal or equitable relief where necessary.
25. EXECUTION
By signing this Agreement, the Parties confirm that they:
have read and understood this Agreement;
have had the opportunity to obtain independent legal or professional advice;
enter into this Agreement voluntarily; and
agree to be legally bound by its terms.
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, including through approved digital signature platforms such as DocuSign. Each signed copy will be deemed an original and together will constitute one Agreement.
